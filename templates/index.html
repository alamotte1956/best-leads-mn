A few things you might be wondering

Is my request a confirmed appointment?

No. Your request must be reviewed and matched. Availability and response times vary.

Do I need an account or a payment card?

No account or card is needed to submit a homeowner request. Discuss any project charges with the contractor before agreeing to work.

What should I check before hiring?

Ask for current licensing, insurance, a written scope, pricing, and payment terms. Choose the contractor that fits your project.

I’m a contractor. Where do I start?

Apply to the Minnesota network. Applications require review before account access and lead eligibility.
