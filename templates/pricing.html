Import authorized pricing evidence

Use account records you are authorized to reuse or a permitted provider feed. Normalize them into the blank CSV template. The template contains no example prices.

  • Supply both actual contractor lead fees in integer USD cents, an observation time including Z or a UTC offset, and private source-document IDs or safe HTTPS references.
  • Use contractor_limit 1 for a comparable single-contractor lead, or 3 for a comparable lead shared with up to three contractors. If sharing or other terms are unknown, do not import it.
  • Verify service, geography, sharing limits, lead quality, billing unit, promotions, subscriptions, taxes and other fees are comparable. Do not use homeowner project estimates, broad averages or unrelated subscription totals.
  • Keep the original records and permission evidence in your secure records. Do not upload customer information, passwords, account numbers, signed URLs or raw invoices.
  • Use a stable import_key for each observation. Identical retries are skipped; changing evidence requires a new key. An invalid row rejects the entire file.


This records source evidence; it does not set an arbitrary platform fee, authorize source access, enable payments, launch a state or activate matching.
