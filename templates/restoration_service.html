Minnesota and Twin Cities Requests

Submit a request for a Minnesota property in Minneapolis, Saint Paul or surrounding communities such as Bloomington, Brooklyn Park, Maple Grove, Plymouth, Woodbury and Eagan. Coverage depends on your property ZIP code, the service needed and contractor availability.

Tell us the property's location even if your mailing address is elsewhere. Requests may require a location review before matching.

Restoration Contractors MN helps homeowners submit requests for restoration and property repair services in the Twin Cities area.

How It Works

1. Tell us what happened and where the property is located.

2. We receive your request and review the service needed.

3. Contractor matching is pending and is not guaranteed.

For immediate danger, leave the affected area and contact the appropriate emergency service.
