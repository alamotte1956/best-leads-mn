Restoration Contractors MN SMS Terms & Conditions

Effective date: October 29, 2026

These terms govern the optional business-to-business SMS program operated by Restoration Contractors MN.

1. Program Description

Participating contractors may receive operational text messages concerning new lead opportunities, lead matching, dispatch, scheduling, lead acceptance and expiration, project status and contractor account alerts. This program does not send promotional or marketing messages. Consumer-facing communications are handled through email.

2. Enrollment and Consent

To enroll, provide a mobile number and affirmatively select the SMS consent checkbox at our contractor registration page. Providing a number or submitting an application without checking the box does not constitute consent. Enrollment is voluntary and is not a condition of registration, purchasing services or doing business with Restoration Contractors MN.

3. Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on available leads, project activity and contractor participation.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Restoration Contractors MN does not charge a separate fee for receiving program text messages. Wireless carrier charges may apply. Separate lead or platform fees, if any, are governed by applicable contractor agreements.

5. Opting Out

Reply STOP to cancel SMS enrollment. We may send one confirmation text acknowledging your request. No further program messages will be sent unless you provide renewed consent. You may also revoke consent through any reasonable method, including contacting us by telephone or mail. Opting out does not automatically terminate your contractor account.

6. Help

Reply HELP for assistance, call 612-701-7064, or visit restorationcontractorsmn.com.

7. Carrier Disclaimer

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Privacy and Data Sharing

We use mobile numbers and SMS consent records to administer the program, deliver authorized notifications and process opt-out requests. Mobile opt-in information and SMS consent data are not sold or shared with third parties, affiliates or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes. Messaging service providers may process mobile numbers solely to operate this program under appropriate confidentiality obligations.

See our Privacy Policy.

9. Changes

We may update these terms. Any changes requiring renewed consent will be handled in accordance with applicable law.
