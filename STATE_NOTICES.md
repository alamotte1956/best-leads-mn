# State consumer notices and Indiana planning

The catalog now includes Minnesota plus six future markets: Texas, Florida, North Carolina, Georgia, Wisconsin and Indiana. Indiana has private state, Indianapolis, Fort Wayne, Northwest Indiana and five restoration-service previews. Only Minnesota remains enabled. Indiana public intake, matching, purchasing and sitemap inclusion remain closed. Migration 023 extends existing state-code constraints to IN without enabling a market or rewriting customer records.

`state_notices.py` is the central, separately sourced notice catalog. `/state-notices` and `/state-notices/<slug>` provide public informational pages. The shared component appears on state/city/service previews, Minnesota home/service/signup, national intake/application templates and the private request-status view. Official references are linked directly in each notice. State notices preserve non-waivable rights and do not substitute for contractor contracts, statutory notices, license scopes, permit requirements, insurance advice or a qualified legal review.

Reviewed against official sources November 2, 2026:

- Minnesota: DLI residential contractor licensing and lookup; Minnesota Statutes 326B.811 roofing cancellation. Distinguish contractor registration from a residential license.
- Texas: Attorney General mold-remediation guidance; Department of Insurance roofing/claim and deductible restrictions. Do not advertise claim representation or deductible waivers.
- Florida: DBPR qualified construction businesses; Department of Financial Services contractor/roofing and insurance consumer protections.
- North Carolina: Licensing Board consumer guidance, including the $40,000 general-contractor threshold; Department of Justice contract/cancellation guidance. Separate trade licensing may apply below the threshold.
- Georgia: Secretary of State specialty-contractor scope/exemptions; Attorney General Consumer Ed. A specialty exemption does not authorize separately regulated trades.
- Wisconsin: DATCP home-improvement requirements and insurance-related exterior-repair protections. Credentials, permits and cancellation rights depend on the work and transaction.
- Indiana: State Business Owner's Guide, PLA credential verification and Monroe County Prosecutor home-improvement guidance. Check local licensing and use an appropriate written agreement.

The privacy text now reflects the actual homeowner choice of one or up to three contractors and potential paid lead access. It no longer suggests that multiple-contractor sharing never occurs. Matched contractor cards show the business-supplied license/registration identifier without implying government endorsement.

This is an engineering/content review, not a legal compliance certification. Before opening another state, obtain state-qualified review of the actual referral/lead fee model, consumer and contractor agreements, applicable advertising/license disclosures, privacy/data rights and consent, insurance-related practices, notices/cancellation procedures and any rewards program. Disclaimers alone cannot correct unlawful operations. Existing draft-policy labels and closed-market/rewards gates remain in place.
