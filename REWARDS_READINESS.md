# LeadIQ Rewards readiness

The owner agreed to leave rewards closed while official rules and legal review are pending. `/contractor/rewards` is an authenticated preparation screen, not an active promotion. It clearly states that purchases do not earn entries or credits and no spins, free entries or redemption are available. All public POST paths for spin, free entry and redemption return 503. No environment flag or form field can override that hold.

`/admin/rewards` lists the outstanding legal/program requirements and business-identity applications. Only active approved contractor accounts can submit an identity-review request. A request cannot self-approve or choose a business ID. The reviewer independently resolves public registry evidence, account authority, aliases, principal legal entity and alternate state registrations. Evidence and private notes are separate from the response visible to the applicant. This is a manual documented review, not an electronic registry connection or automated authoritative identity check. An identifier's hash alone is not proof of identity.

Database uniqueness permits one contractor account per verified reward business and one business per contractor account. A principal registry jurisdiction plus normalized public registration identifier produces the business identity digest. Duplicate/format-alias requests cannot create a second linked account. Human review is still required to resolve other-state registrations or aliases with different identifiers to the same legal entity. Renewal can extend verification only for the same business; it cannot switch business identity or reset its monthly counter. Identity decisions, links and generic email notices commit together. Replays cannot add another decision. Expired verification, revoked business identity or revoked contractor approval fail closed in account readiness.

Migration 013 registers business, credit, month, spin, identity application, account-link and review tables without issuing any credits or spins. `reward_transactions.py` remains a **simulation/internal primitive**, disconnected from live purchases and entries. It uses a server random selector with eight placeholder outcomes, not approved prizes. Both entry methods call that same placeholder selector. Sources are globally unique; credits belong to a business and cannot be transferred or consumed twice. A conditional combined counter caps consumption at 10 per UTC calendar month in the prototype. First-month creation uses a database upsert, with PostgreSQL row locks and conditional updates or SQLite writer serialization. Callers must roll back any failed transaction.

Do not connect those primitives to a public endpoint or provider callback before the official rules define eligibility, calendar time zone, qualifying purchase criteria, equal no-purchase entry process, approved prizes/weights, inventory and fulfillment. A signed successful purchase alone is not yet a legally defined qualifying purchase. There is intentionally no production credit issuer, approved free-entry issuer or real redemption flow until those decisions are provided. Never accept a client-supplied purchase status or free-entry approval.

Automated checks cover manual review/ownership/CSRF, evidence requirements, duplicate identities/accounts, expiration/revocation, renewal preserving exhausted monthly counters, private evidence isolation, transaction rollback and closed live endpoints. Primitive tests cover globally replayed sources, transfer/double-spend rejection, mixed purchase/free monthly cap and rollover, identical server selection, revoked business and concurrency. Eighteen concurrent mixed credits in a new month yield exactly ten committed spins; eight simultaneous attempts on one credit yield exactly one. The PostgreSQL CI run passed mixed monthly-cap and source-replay concurrency checks. The full migration check and real promotion/fulfillment tests remain required before any launch.
