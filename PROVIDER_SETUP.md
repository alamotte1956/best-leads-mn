## Contractor-reported follow-up

`/contractor/leads` lists only the authenticated contractor's accepted offers, with stage/date filters and 20-row pagination. Each accepted offer has a private follow-up page for reported stage, an optional next date and a note (2,000-character maximum). Dates use America/Chicago for dashboard organization; no automated reminders, calendar sync or email/SMS are scheduled by these entries. Accepted historical offers can be logged after matching stops, but pending/declined offers and another contractor's offers cannot be accessed through this workflow.

Current progress and append-only update history share a transaction. Revision checks reject stale edits while preserving the draft and showing the latest saved state; an intentional subsequent save creates another history record. Identical retries do not duplicate reports. Administrators have a read-only view linked from request review. Notes are shared only with that contractor and administrators and are omitted from consumer tracking and messages. These reports do not prove an appointment or completion, close matching, alter an offer/payment, issue credits or fulfill a reward. Obtain independent evidence for any later fulfillment workflow.

Contact actions encode email addresses as recipients rather than mailto header/query parameters and reject control characters. This is applied to both consumer and contractor contact buttons. References: [RFC 6068](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6068), [Python URL quoting](https://docs.python.org/3/library/urllib.parse.html#urllib.parse.quote). This does not verify ownership or deliverability of an email address.

`/admin/integrations` shows enabled switches, missing configuration names and recent delivery/payment statuses without showing key values. Account presence is not proof of provider verification or successful live operation.

## Resend

Resend is the selected email provider for both consumers and contractors. Consumer request receipts, contractor application receipts, account approval/deactivation/rejection notices, contractor lead-offer notifications and contractor payment confirmations use the same Resend outbox. Consumer email remains optional; SMS delivery is separate. This integration sends transactional notifications; two-way inbox/reply handling and marketing campaigns are not implemented.

Consumers who supplied an email also receive acceptance, closure and cancellation updates. These notices contain a request reference and generic status, without homeowner names, phone numbers, ZIPs, project descriptions or contractor contact details. Their status-page link is not an email login or transferable magic link. It opens details only in the browser that submitted the latest request, for seven days. Switching devices, clearing cookies or an older pre-update session requires a future verified recovery workflow; request numbers and email addresses cannot retrieve private records. No consumer SMS was added.

`/request-status` uses the server-signed submission session and has private/no-store, no-referrer and noindex headers. It displays live matching state and accepted business contacts only while the assigned contractor remains active, approved and currently eligible. Multiple legacy accepted offers or an ineligible matched account show a review-needed state without contact details. Closed/cancelled matching is distinct from project completion or appointment confirmation. Consumer identity/contact/project-description fields and internal contractor fees are omitted.

Consumer update records, offer acceptance or administrative closure/cancellation, pending-offer changes and queue entries share a transaction. Later request statuses cancel older pending notices; the worker rechecks current status, match eligibility and the original recipient before dispatch. An already-sending message cannot be recalled. The per-request administrative view shows the latest 20 consumer update statuses. Reopening/reassigning a request is not implemented; do not bypass the one-update-per-state constraints with manual database edits.

Consumers can stop matching for their own latest request during the same seven-day browser access window. A separate review page requires CSRF validation, explicit confirmation and a reference that must match the current browser-owned request. The reference is compared only after ownership validation; it cannot select another record. This prevents an old confirmation tab from cancelling a newer submission. GET never changes the request, and repeated/concurrent confirmations do not duplicate updates.

Consumer cancellation and administrator closure/cancellation share the lead lock, conditional transition, pending-offer cancellation, role-based audit and email transaction. Accepted offers and payment records remain intact. The administrative request page shows the latest 20 status changes with the authenticated actor role, independent of whether the consumer supplied an email. Existing arrangements, financial records and Stripe sessions are not cancelled by the consumer control.

Account decisions, append-only activity records and their notices commit in one database transaction. Repeating an unchanged approval/deactivation does not enqueue another notice. Repeated rejected reviews retain review history without repeating an unchanged rejection notice. Deactivation cancels pending offers and retains accepted offers and payments. Later account decisions cancel older pending notices. Before sending, the worker rechecks the current decision, account state, recipient address and credentials. In-flight email cannot be recalled; use the current account state as authoritative. Changing account contact addresses requires reconciliation, not redirecting a queued notice automatically.

Approved contractors can request an additional catalog service from service settings, and withdraw an unresolved request belonging to their own account. Requests do not change matching. One pending request per contractor/service is enforced by a unique reservation key; withdrawal or rejection releases it while retaining history. An administrator reviews requests at `/admin/service-requests`. Approval requires an active approved non-test account, a current recorded license/insurance review, explicit service-scope checks and a coverage evidence reference. Approval adds a ContractorService row without deleting existing rows or changing the primary service. Removal and primary-service changes are not implemented.

Request receipts and recorded review decisions queue generic Resend notices in the same transaction. A decision notice describes the historical request outcome, not a continuing guarantee of current eligibility. Internal review notes and coverage references stay administrator-only and out of emails; rejection requires a separate message visible to the contractor. Read-only history remains in service settings, and resubmissions after rejection/withdrawal create new records. Service requests cannot approve an account, start a purchase or change pricing. Registry/insurer checks remain manual; this is not an external verification integration.

Deactivation and rejection notices may reach inactive accounts only through their authenticated administrator-created activity record. Ordinary lead notifications still require an approved, active, verified contractor. Private credential-review notes and evidence references are excluded from notice bodies. The credential-review screen shows the latest 20 account decisions with their email statuses. Existing approvals are not backfilled or emailed. Contractors create and reset their own passwords through /contractor/forgot-password. Approval notices point to this self-service page; administrators cannot issue password setup links or choose contractor passwords. An approval notice does not authorize a payment or guarantee lead availability. Confirm a working support contact on the branded website before enabling these notices.

Verify the sender domain in Resend and configure its required DNS records. Set RESEND_API_KEY and RESEND_FROM_EMAIL to an authorized sender, then enable EMAIL_SENDING_ENABLED only for the intended environment. Homeowner request receipts (where an email was supplied), application receipts, lead-offer emails and payment confirmations are queued transactionally.

The worker calls https://api.resend.com/emails with a stable Idempotency-Key and a server-owned delivery_id tag. `sent` means provider acceptance. Resend idempotency keys expire after 24 hours, so uncertain messages are never automatically retried.

Register the public HTTPS endpoint `/webhooks/resend` in the intended Resend account for email.sent, email.delivery_delayed, email.delivered, email.failed, email.bounced, email.complained and email.suppressed. Store that endpoint's signing secret in RESEND_WEBHOOK_SECRET. The endpoint verifies raw-body Svix signatures with constant-time HMAC comparison, a five-minute attempt-timestamp tolerance and durable message-ID deduplication. Keep the server clock synchronized. It rejects mismatched recipients/tags, limits payload size and ignores unrelated account mail.

Delivery updates, audit events and recipient blocks commit together. Server-owned tags correlate callbacks arriving before the send response; later send completion or timeout cannot overwrite terminal callback status. Status priority prevents late sent/delayed/delivered events from clearing failures, hard bounces, suppression or complaints. `delivered` means acceptance by the receiving mail server, not reading or inbox placement. Email opens/clicks are not tracked.

Permanent bounces, spam complaints and provider suppression create durable local blocks for future consumer AND contractor messages, checked before sending. Explicit temporary bounces and delays do not permanently block an address; generic failures require reconciliation and are not retried automatically. Blocks use the same trimmed, lowercase address normalization as intake, without inventing alias rules. `/admin/integrations` shows the blocked count and latest 50 addresses/reasons. No automatic unblock or blind resend action exists. Retain first-block provenance and all linked event history.

Emails already in flight cannot be recalled. Keep Resend's own account suppression enabled for send/callback races, import or reconcile pre-existing provider suppressions before release, and inspect provider records before any manual correction. Changing a contact address must not remove the old address block. Legacy messages without correlation tags require manual reconciliation if the callback arrives before the provider ID was saved. Two-way inbound email, administrative suppression correction and real provider delivery testing remain outstanding.

## Worker and uncertainty

Schedule `flask --app app deliver-notifications` in the approved environment. It handles up to 20 pending records per invocation. Multiple invocations claim messages using conditional database updates. Separate channel switches default off. Matching queues offer notifications in the same transaction as creating an offer. Existing matching activation controls remain unchanged.

Approved contractors can independently pause email/SMS lead-offer alerts at `/contractor/notifications`. No saved preference retains existing channel defaults, subject to consent, suppression and provider switches. Pausing alerts does not change matching, approval, service permissions or existing offers. Account, application, service-review, consumer and payment emails retain their existing controls. Turning alerts back on affects future offers; previously suppressed outbox records are not revived. Preferences cannot grant SMS consent, remove STOP or email blocks, or enable provider switches.

Preference saves, append-only change history and suppression of pending lead alerts share a transaction. Revision checks prevent a stale form from overwriting another session's changes. Identical retries do not add history records. The queue filters preferences, and the worker checks them again before claiming and immediately before sending. The authoritative SMS eligibility function also enforces SMS preferences for the legacy guarded dispatch helper. Its read-only consent inspection option must not be used by senders. Already-in-flight messages can still arrive; provider-side suppression remains necessary.

Administrators can inspect current choices, consent/block status and the latest ten changes at `/admin/contractors/<id>/notifications`, but cannot override them through that endpoint. The controls, settings and operating pages use private/no-store headers. No existing accounts are backfilled or opted into SMS by migration 010. Test PostgreSQL write races and real provider pause/resume behavior in isolated staging before release.

A provider timeout or process crash after claiming a message can leave `uncertain` or `sending`. Inspect the provider's message records before manually reconciling such a record; do not blindly set it back to pending. The worker prioritizes avoiding duplicate texts over automatic retry availability. No public send endpoint or open email relay exists. Provider-side suppression is still required for opt-out races.

## Stripe Checkout

Contractors must be approved, active and verified, with current recorded credentials, before accessing billing, requesting quotes or starting checkout. The existing server-side session guard rechecks the account on every request. Registration, an old session, an accepted offer or a saved quote cannot bypass approval. New administrative approvals require a current approved license/insurance review. Existing approved legacy accounts retain their recorded approval status until reviewed; audit them before release.

New checkout uses daily market observations, not assigned Stripe Price mappings. The fee is 85% of the lower comparable Angi/Thumbtack contractor lead fee, rounded down. Evidence must match service, state and ZIP and the current Central calendar day. The daily source adapter is NOT connected; missing evidence blocks quoting. `/admin/pricing` displays observations and readiness without editing prices. See MARKET_PRICING.md for source-access requirements, quote expiry and the trusted refresh command. Set a mode-matching STRIPE_SECRET_KEY, STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET, HTTPS origin PUBLIC_SITE_URL and timezone-aware PAYMENTS_ACTIVATION_UTC. Start in STRIPE_MODE=test with PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true for sandbox tests. Live keys additionally require STRIPE_MODE=live and STRIPE_LIVE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=true.

Only the owner of an accepted offer can request a quote and initiate checkout, and its accepted_at must be on/after the configured activation date. Historical or undated offers are excluded. A saved, expiring quote precedes explicit payment; no automatic charges occur. Stripe receives inline USD price_data from that stored quote, with dynamic payment methods. Historical Minnesota acceptance access is retained. Newly billable accepted offers remain contact-locked until a signed successful payment has been recorded; returning from checkout does not unlock contact. Test orders cannot unlock live contacts or be reused for live checkout. Full refunds remove subsequent contact access, although previously viewed data cannot be recalled. Ownership and an idempotency key control session creation; client-supplied amounts are ignored.

New website quotes and checkout also require the request to remain New/open; closed or cancelled requests are excluded from billable offers. Existing payment history and signed payment/refund callbacks are retained. A previously created Stripe-hosted session can still exist outside the website: session expiration/reconciliation must be completed and tested before live payments are enabled. Stopping matching does not initiate a refund, void an existing payment or recall an in-flight checkout request.

Register /webhooks/stripe for checkout.session.expired, checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded, checkout.session.async_payment_failed and charge.refunded. The handler checks the raw-body signature, test/live mode, linked session, metadata, amount, currency and paid status. Unpaid completion waits for successful payment. Browser return URLs never confirm payment. Distinct duplicate paid events cannot issue duplicate receipts or roll a refunded payment back to paid. Refund totals are monotonic. No rewards credits or prizes are issued by this integration.

Expired/closed checkout reconciliation and versioned quote replacement are implemented behind the default-off CHECKOUT_RECONCILIATION_ENABLED switch; see CHECKOUT_RECONCILIATION.md and migration 019. Real sandbox validation, disputes and refund initiation still require follow-up handling. Do not retry a checkout with a new order or arbitrary database edits; inspect Stripe first. No subscription or auto-purchase flow is included.
